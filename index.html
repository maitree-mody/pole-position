Systematic trading for prediction markets.

Pole Position is an independent project building automated, data-driven trading systems for regulated event-contract exchanges, finding high-probability opportunities and executing them with strict risk controls.

What we do

Live data pipelines

Real-time market, sports and economic data feeds, recorded and stored for research and backtesting.

Strategy research

Every idea is tested against historical data and independently verified before it trades. Most don't make the cut.

Automated execution

Always-on trading engines with position limits, sizing rules and paper-trading stages before any capital is at risk.

How it works

1 · Research

AI-assisted agents explore market data and propose candidate edges.

2 · Verify

Separate checks reproduce results and stress-test them for fill realism and hidden risk.

3 · Paper → live

Strategies run on paper against live prices, then graduate to small real sizes only after proving out.

About

Pole Position is an early-stage project run by a two-person founding team. We're building the infrastructure to research and run prediction-market strategies reliably, around the clock.
